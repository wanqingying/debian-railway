# Report Template

Use this structure for the deliverable. Keep it in the user's language. Omit a section only when it is genuinely empty, and say why.

The header block is not decoration: a reader who sees the accuracy figure and the lane mix knows immediately how much of this report they can re-check themselves.

```markdown
# <Title — the question, answered in a phrase>

**Question:** <the user's question, restated precisely>
**Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>  **Depth:** quick | standard | deep
**Sources:** <N> (<n1> primary) · **Citation accuracy:** <pct>% (<supported>/<checked>) · **In-window:** <n1>/<n2> dated (<pct>%) · **Undated:** <n3> · **Cutoff:** <window searched>

## TL;DR
- <3–6 bullets: the conclusions a busy reader needs, each with a citation>
- <state the single biggest uncertainty here, not at the end>

## Method
- Nodes investigated: <N1…N5, from outline.json>
- Source families and lanes used: <e.g. GitHub releases + official docs (public), vendor pricing pages (render), G2 reviews (authenticated)>
- What was not available: <paywalls, logins, rate limits — and how that bounds the result>
- Spend: <sources, workers, minutes; what the budget cut short>

## Findings

<Prose. Every non-obvious claim gets an inline citation [S1]. Keep facts and your analysis visually
```

## Rules

- **Inline citations, next to claims.** A reader must reach a claim and its source together; a bare reference list at the end is not enough.
- **Ids come from the register.** `evidence.mjs render` assigns `S#` by first use and writes `sources.json`; hand-numbering drifts and breaks the audit.
- **Cite the primary source.** If a secondary source points at a paper/dataset/filing, cite that too.
- **Two dates per source.** `Published` is when the page was written; `Retrieved` is when you fetched it. A source published in 2024 and fetched today is a 2024 source, and the header's in-window figure is what keeps that visible. Never substitute one for the other — a fresh retrieval cannot make an old fact current.
- **Dates everywhere.** Report date, both dates per source, and the recency window for "latest" questions.
- **No orphan claims.** Anything non-obvious without a citation is either cited or moved into Analysis as a labelled inference.
- **The accuracy number stays even when it is unflattering.** An admitted 88% is a better artifact than an unmeasured claim of completeness.
- **Length follows the question**, not a page target. Density beats volume; never pad.
- **File output**: `docs/research/<slug>-<YYYY-MM-DD>/report.md`, with `outline.json`, `evidence.jsonl`, `sources.json`, `claims.json` beside it, unless the user asked for another location or format.
