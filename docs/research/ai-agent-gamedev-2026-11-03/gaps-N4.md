No peer-reviewed RCT or studio-scale deployment study of a code assistant *inside* Unity/Unreal/Godot was found; industry GDC survey numbers (36% genAI use, role splits) belong to node N1 and were not duplicated here.
- **Paduraru et al. (Procedia CS 2024) "LLM-based methods for the creation of unit tests in game development"** could not be retrieved (ScienceDirect PDF download failed), so its coverage-improvement numbers are not cited.
